Profile

Scholar of China and the world order, programme builder, and adviser to officers, executives and policymakers.

PhDPeking University2023–24Fulbright–Nehru Fellow2015joined IIM Indore

He studies the changing nature of global order, and translates it for the people who have to act inside it: officers, executives and policymakers.

Professor G. Venkat Raman teaches and researches geopolitics, global governance, international political economy and China studies at the Indian Institute of Management Indore. His work connects great-power rivalry to its consequences for supply chains, industrial strategy and international business.

He received his PhD from the School of Government at Peking University and did postdoctoral research at Fudan University. He holds the Fulbright–Nehru Academic and Professional Excellence Fellowship (2023–24) and the Nehru Memorial Fellowship, and is an Honorary Fellow of the Institute of Chinese Studies, New Delhi.

He was Principal Investigator of an ICSSR major research project on BRICS as an alternative vision of trans-regional cooperation from the Global South, and sits on the ICSSR Expert Committee on China Studies and the Advisory Council of the Organisation for Research on China and Asia.
